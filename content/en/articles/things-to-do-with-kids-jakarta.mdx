Jakarta doesn't always get credit as a family destination, but scratch the surface and there's a huge amount to do with kids — world-class aquariums, a giant zoo, theme parks, museums, and endless indoor play for those tropical downpours.

This guide gathers the best **things to do with kids in Jakarta**, from big-ticket attractions to easy everyday outings, whether you live here or you're visiting with the family.

## Top Family Attractions in Jakarta

* **Ragunan Zoo** (South Jakarta) — a vast, leafy 147-hectare zoo home to thousands of animals, including orangutans and Komodo dragons. Tickets are cheap, making it a classic budget-friendly family day.
* **Jakarta Aquarium & Safari** (Neo Soho, West Jakarta) — an indoor aquarium and mini-safari with live shows, right inside a mall — ideal when the weather turns.
* **SeaWorld Ancol** (North Jakarta) — a large aquarium within the Ancol complex, with a huge main tank and touch pools.
* **Ancol Dreamland** (North Jakarta) — the big bayfront recreation complex, home to **Dunia Fantasi (Dufan)** theme park, **Atlantis Water Adventure**, SeaWorld, and beaches.
* **Taman Mini Indonesia Indah (TMII)** (East Jakarta) — a cultural park with mini-replicas of traditional houses from across Indonesia, museums, a cable car, and Snowbay waterpark.
* **KidZania Jakarta** (Pacific Place, SCBD) — a role-play "city" where kids try nearly 100 grown-up jobs; equal parts fun and educational.

<Callout type="tip">
  Jakarta traffic can eat a whole morning. Plan outings for **weekday mornings** where possible, cluster attractions in the same area, and always have a rainy-day (indoor) backup in mind.
</Callout>

## Indoor Play & Rainy-Day Ideas

Between the heat and the afternoon rain, indoor options are a lifesaver. Mall-based soft-play centres and role-play parks — KidZania, Miniapolis, SuperPark, Kiztopia, Kidzoona — keep kids happy for hours in air-conditioned comfort.

For the full list of indoor centres and outdoor parks, see our dedicated guide to the [best playgrounds in Jakarta for kids](/en/articles/playgrounds-jakarta-kids).

## Parks & Outdoor Fun

When mornings are cool and dry, Jakarta's parks come into their own — **Tebet Eco Park**, **Taman Menteng**, **Taman Suropati**, and the city's many free RPTRA playgrounds give kids space to run, cycle, and play in the shade. Many are completely free (some, like Tebet Eco Park, just need free app registration).

## Cool Off & Refuel

On a hot day, Jakarta's [water parks](/en/articles/water-parks-jakarta) — Waterbom PIK, Snowbay, and Atlantis — are a sure win with kids. And when everyone's hungry, the city's [kid-friendly cafés and restaurants with play areas](/en/articles/kid-friendly-cafes-restaurants-jakarta) let parents enjoy a proper meal while the children play.

## Museums & Learning

For a change of pace, Jakarta has engaging museums for families — from **Museum MACAN** (modern and contemporary art, with interactive installations kids love) to the **National Museum** and the science and history exhibits around Kota Tua (Old Town), where kids can also rent colourful bikes.

## Make a Weekend of It

Turn a day out into a family staycation: many Jakarta hotels offer kids clubs, pools, and family packages that pair perfectly with a nearby attraction. See our round-up of [kid-friendly hotels in Jakarta](/en/articles/kid-friendly-hotels-jakarta) to pick a base.

## Everyday Outings for Expat Families

Settling in with young kids? Beyond the big attractions, the day-to-day matters just as much:

* Find reliable care — our guides to [daycare in Jakarta](/en/articles/daycare-jakarta-expat-guide) and [preschools & kindergartens in Jakarta](/en/articles/preschools-kindergartens-jakarta) cover options, costs, and the best areas for expat families.

## FAQs — Things to Do with Kids in Jakarta

<FAQ question="What are the best things to do with kids in Jakarta?">
  Family favourites include Ragunan Zoo, Jakarta Aquarium & Safari, SeaWorld and Dufan at Ancol, TMII, and KidZania, plus indoor play centres and free parks like Tebet Eco Park. Mix a big attraction with an indoor backup in case of rain.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="What can you do with kids in Jakarta when it rains?">
  Rainy days are ideal for indoor play centres (KidZania, Miniapolis, SuperPark, Kiztopia), the Jakarta Aquarium & Safari at Neo Soho, SeaWorld Ancol, and family-friendly museums like Museum MACAN — all indoors and air-conditioned.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Is Jakarta good for a family holiday?">
  Yes — Jakarta has zoos, aquariums, theme parks, museums, and abundant indoor play, plus many family-friendly hotels for staycations. The main things to plan around are traffic and tropical weather, so cluster nearby attractions and keep an indoor backup.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="What free activities are there for kids in Jakarta?">
  Many of Jakarta's parks and playgrounds are free, including Tebet Eco Park, Taman Menteng, Taman Suropati, and the city's RPTRA child-friendly public spaces. Ragunan Zoo is also very inexpensive.
</FAQ>
