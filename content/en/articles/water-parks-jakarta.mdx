Few things beat Jakarta's heat like a day at a water park — and the city has some big, well-run ones with everything from gentle toddler pools to stomach-dropping slides. Whether you're after a relaxed splash with a two-year-old or thrills for older kids, there's a park to suit.

This guide covers the best **water parks in Jakarta for families**, what each offers for different ages, and practical tips for a smooth day out. For more ideas, see our [things to do with kids in Jakarta](/en/articles/things-to-do-with-kids-jakarta) guide.

## Best Water Parks in Jakarta

### Waterbom Jakarta (PIK)

In **Pantai Indah Kapuk (North Jakarta)**, Waterbom is a big, well-maintained park with slides and pools for a range of ages, clean facilities, and changing rooms. Tickets typically include a life jacket for children and swim rings — handy for younger swimmers.

### Snowbay Waterpark (TMII)

Set inside **Taman Mini Indonesia Indah (East Jakarta)**, Snowbay has a fun snowy-mountain theme with **wave pools, a lazy river, and big slides** like Everslide and Typhoon River. Crucially for families, it has well-kept **toddler pools and mini water playgrounds** (the Mimi Family zone), so little ones have their own safe space while older kids tackle the Hurricane Slide and Flush Bowl.

### Atlantis Water Adventure (Ancol)

Part of the huge **Ancol Dreamland** complex on the bayfront, Atlantis spans over five hectares with an underwater-city theme. It has **pools for different age groups**, including a wave pool and a variety of slides, and pairs easily with Ancol's other attractions (SeaWorld, Dufan) for a big family day.

<Callout type="tip">
  Go on a **weekday** to dodge the biggest crowds, arrive at opening while it's cooler, and bring reef shoes, extra sunscreen, and a change of clothes. Check whether life jackets and rings are included or rented — it varies by park.
</Callout>

## Practical Tips for a Water Park Day

* **Arrive early** — beat the heat, the crowds, and the queues for popular slides.
* **Pack the essentials** — swimwear, reef shoes, waterproof sunscreen, towels, and a change of clothes.
* **Mind the little ones** — head for the dedicated toddler pools and stay within arm's reach.
* **Check height/age rules** for the bigger slides before you queue.

## FAQs — Water Parks in Jakarta

<FAQ question="What is the best water park in Jakarta?">
  The most popular water parks in Jakarta are Waterbom in PIK (North Jakarta), Snowbay at TMII (East Jakarta), and Atlantis Water Adventure at Ancol. Waterbom and Atlantis are great all-rounders, while Snowbay is especially strong for toddlers thanks to its dedicated mini pools.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Are Jakarta water parks suitable for toddlers?">
  Yes. Snowbay TMII in particular has well-kept toddler pools and mini water playgrounds, and most parks have shallow zones. Bring a life jacket (some parks include one) and stay within arm's reach of very young children.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="When is the best time to visit a water park in Jakarta?">
  Weekday mornings are best — smaller crowds, shorter slide queues, and cooler temperatures. Weekends and school holidays get busy, so arrive at opening if you go then.
</FAQ>
