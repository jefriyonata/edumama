Whether you're visiting Jakarta with children or just after a weekend "staycation" — a hugely popular local escape — the right hotel can make all the difference. A kids club, a good pool, and a family room turn a night away into a genuine holiday for everyone.

This guide rounds up the best **kid-friendly hotels in Jakarta**, what makes a hotel family-friendly, and a few tips for booking. Planning the days around it? See our [things to do with kids in Jakarta](/en/articles/things-to-do-with-kids-jakarta) guide.

## What Makes a Hotel Kid-Friendly?

Before you book, look for:

* **A kids club** — supervised or drop-in play spaces buy parents a much-needed break.
* **A good pool** — ideally with a shallow children's section or mini water-play area.
* **Family or connecting rooms** — space matters, and connecting rooms are gold for slightly older kids.
* **Children's dining** — a kids' menu, high chairs, and flexible breakfast hours.
* **Location** — close to the attractions or malls you plan to visit, to dodge Jakarta traffic.

### Mercure Ancol

Sitting near the Ancol recreation complex in **North Jakarta**, Mercure Ancol is a favourite for families thanks to its **MIMO Kids Club** and a **children's pool with a mini waterpark**. Its location makes it easy to pair with SeaWorld, Dufan, and the bayfront.

### Hotel Borobudur Jakarta

A **Central Jakarta** classic set in lush landscaped gardens, Hotel Borobudur is known for its **expansive pool** and generous grounds — rare breathing room in the middle of the city, and plenty of space for kids to roam.

### Sheraton Grand Jakarta Gandaria City

Attached to a major **South Jakarta** mall, the Sheraton Grand runs family-oriented programming — think crafts, activities, and family-friendly dining — making it an easy, all-in-one staycation base with shopping and cinemas downstairs.

### DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta

Well located in **Central Jakarta**, DoubleTree offers a **Kids Club** with slides and a ball pool, a good pool, and famously warm welcome cookies — a reliable, comfortable family choice.

### Novotel Jakarta Pulomas

In **East Jakarta**, Novotel Pulomas is a spacious, relaxed option with **family rooms, a pool, and a Kids Club** — handy for families visiting the TMII/eastern side of the city.

### Raffles Jakarta

For a **luxury** treat in **South Jakarta (Kuningan)**, Raffles offers elegant family rooms with children's amenities and impeccable service — a special-occasion staycation.

<Callout type="tip">
  "Staycation" packages are big in Jakarta, especially on weekends and school holidays. Booking **directly with the hotel** often unlocks family perks — kids stay/eat free, late checkout, or kids-club access — that third-party sites don't show.
</Callout>

## Tips for Booking a Family Hotel in Jakarta

* **Match the hotel to your plans.** Ancol-area hotels suit theme-park days; central hotels suit sightseeing and malls.
* **Ask about age limits** for kids-eat-free and kids-club access — they vary by hotel.
* **Request connecting or family rooms early** — they sell out on weekends and holidays.
* **Check pool hours and depth** if you have very young swimmers.

## FAQs — Kid-Friendly Hotels in Jakarta

<FAQ question="Which hotels in Jakarta have a kids club?">

  Very. Weekend hotel staycations are a popular local escape, and many hotels offer family packages with kids-club access, children dining free, and late checkout — especially on weekends and during school holidays.
</FAQ>

<FAQ question="Do Jakarta hotels have family rooms?">
  Yes. Many hotels offer family or connecting rooms; Novotel Jakarta Pulomas and Raffles Jakarta are among those with dedicated family accommodation. Book early, as family rooms sell out fast on weekends.
</FAQ>
